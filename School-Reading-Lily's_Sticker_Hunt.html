Lily's Sticker Hunt! 🎒

Story Book Edition
Page 1
Lily is a student. She sits at her desk. She loves stickers! She has many cute stickers. But look! Lily is sad. "Where is my favorite bear sticker?" she asks.
Page 2
Lily stands up and looks around. She looks at her chair. There is a pink eraser and a blue marker on the chair. But the bear sticker is not there! "It is not on my chair," Lily says.
Page 3
Her friend, Ken, wants to help. Ken looks under the big table. There are some colorful books under the table. Ken lifts a yellow book. "Is it under this book, Lily?" Ken asks. "No, it isn't," Lily says.
Page 4
Ms. Green comes over with a big smile. "Let's check the box, Lily," Ms. Green says. Lily opens the purple box. She looks inside. There is an orange notebook and a green glue in the box. "It is not in the box," Lily sighs.
Page 5
Everyone is looking for the bear sticker. Lily looks at Ms. Green's desk. There is a computer and a thick red dictionary on the desk. "Look!" Ken cries. "There is a wooden ruler under the desk!" "But no sticker."
Page 6
Lily walks back to her seat. She opens her pink school bag. "Oh!" Lily smiles big. The bear sticker is in her school bag! "Thank you, Ms. Green! Thank you, Ken!" Lily is very happy now!
