Sarah Asks for Help 🎒

Story Book Edition
Page 1
Look! This is Sarah. She sits at her desk. She is wearing a blue T-shirt today.
Page 2
Listen! The teacher is talking. "Please do page five," the teacher says.
Page 3
Everyone is working. Sarah looks at her paper. She is reading the words. But wait! She does not understand. The work is very hard. "I am stuck," Sarah says. She stays very quiet.
Page 4
"I am stuck..." The minutes are passing. Sarah looks at her friends. They are writing quickly. She wants to finish her work too.
Page 5
Sarah takes a deep breath. She lifts her arm. Look! She raises her hand high in the air!
Page 6
The teacher smiles and walks over. "Yes, Sarah?" the teacher asks. "I am stuck," Sarah says. "Can you help me, please?" "Yes, of course!" the teacher says. Sarah is smiling now. She is happy. She can do it!
